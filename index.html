Bare Metal

Before you sign an Atlanta hood-cleaning quote, ask whether the job stops at the filters or goes to bare metal on the hood, duct, and fan — and whether you get a dated sticker.

Restaurant owners keep getting two prices for what sounds like the same night: one crew washes filters and wipes the canopy; another scrapes the plenum, accessible duct, and rooftop fan and leaves a certificate. Fire marshals and insurers usually want the second file. This page is that checklist, plus phone numbers copied from each shop’s own site.

Atlanta operator list · Questions owners actually ask

Ask on the quote

  1. Hood, filters, plenum, accessible duct, and exhaust fan — or filters only?
  2. Cleaned to bare metal, or degreased where the line can see it?
  3. Dated sticker on the hood, plus a written note of sections they could not reach?
  4. Night or after-close, and who covers the line before they wash?
  5. Is suppression service (nozzles, links) a different company and a different date?
